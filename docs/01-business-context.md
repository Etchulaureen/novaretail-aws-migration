# 01 — Business Context

## Client

**NovaRetail France** is a fictional mid-sized retailer with a public web platform and internal business services.

## Current problem

The company relies on ageing on-premises infrastructure. Main risks:

- Hardware replacement cost
- Limited scalability
- Local-only backups
- Weak disaster recovery
- Limited monitoring
- Frequent maintenance windows
- Growing technical debt

## Business objectives

1. Improve availability and resilience
2. Reduce hardware dependency
3. Strengthen monitoring and security
4. Create room for growth
5. Standardise infrastructure provisioning
6. Improve backup and recovery

## Success criteria

- No data loss
- Critical tests pass
- Downtime remains inside the approved window
- Monitoring and security controls are active
- Business owner signs off
- Rollback remains possible until acceptance
